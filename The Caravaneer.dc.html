THE CARAVANEER

“Bright moons, traveler. This one trades in wisdom — sets, skills, and the old stories — and asks nothing but your patience in return.”

An in-game guide who is also a character: a Khajiit trickster-merchant who, beneath the patter, is a scholar and old warrior. Below is the design system and three visual directions for the companion's mobile chat — meant to be mixed and matched.

The Soul · Trickster-Sage

A fool's grin worn by a merchant — and under the merchant, a quiet scholar who has seen a hundred wars. Helpful, sly, never breaking character; sharp on the numbers, soft in the telling.

Speaks asthird person — “this one,” “Zaharra” — rarely “I.” Ends with a soft “yes?”
